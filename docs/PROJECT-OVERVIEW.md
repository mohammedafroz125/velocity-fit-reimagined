# Velocity Fit — Project Overview

## 1. Executive Summary
**Velocity Fit** (`https://velocityfit.in/`) is a Hyderabad-based wellness, weight-loss, and clinical nutrition brand founded and led by **Dr. Lubna Fatima**. The brand currently acquires a significant portion of its clientele via Instagram (`@velocityfit.official`) and direct peer communication.

The primary mission of Velocity Fit is to promote metabolic health, reverse lifestyle-induced obesity, and contribute toward an "Obesity-Free India" through medically grounded, sustainable nutrition and habit engineering rather than extreme deprivation diets or fads.

## 2. Business Objectives
1. **Transition to an Owned Digital Asset:** Shift dependence from third-party social algorithms (Instagram) to a high-converting, authority-building web platform.
2. **Measurable Lead Acquisition:** Convert qualified visitors into leads using structured assessments (e.g., the Pre-Signup Health Assessment / V6 Audit™ Foundation) and consultative consultation bookings.
3. **Streamlined Practice Operations:** Provide a non-technical administration interface for Dr. Lubna Fatima and the clinical team to manage inbound leads, track consultation progress, and update content effortlessly.
4. **Attribution & Marketing ROI:** Capture granular campaign attribution (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, landing pages) to allocate marketing spend with precision.

## 4. Target User Personas
### Persona A: The Frustrated Chronic Dieter (New Visitor)
- **Profile:** 28–48 years old, urban professional or homemaker dealing with stubborn weight gain, visceral fat, low energy, PCOS, or pre-diabetes.
- **Pain Points:** Tried multiple commercial crash diets or gyms with rebound weight gain; confused by conflicting online fitness advice.
- **Goal on Platform:** Discover why previous attempts failed, gain confidence in Dr. Fatima’s clinical approach, and complete an initial assessment.

### Persona B: The High-Risk Clinical Patient (Referred / Medical)
- **Profile:** Experiencing diagnosed metabolic conditions (Type 2 Diabetes, Fatty Liver, Hypertension, Thyroid dysfunction).
- **Pain Points:** Needs medical-grade nutritional supervision that is safe, evidence-backed, and coordinated with their health markers.
- **Goal on Platform:** Review clinical credentials, understand the medical protocol, and book a direct consultation.

### Persona C: Existing Active Customer
- **Goal on Platform:** Quick access to consultation links, support channels, program guidelines, and resources.

### Persona D: Non-Technical Business Owner / Admin (Dr. Lubna Fatima & Team)
- **Goal on Platform:** Zero-friction management of incoming leads, viewing questionnaire submissions, updating consultation availability, and maintaining FAQs without writing code.
